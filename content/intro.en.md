I am a research professor at the IBS Center for Molecular Spectroscopy and Dynamics, Korea University. My research focuses on developing and applying **asynchronous and interferometric nonlinear spectroscopy (AI-NS)** and optical-frequency-comb techniques to investigate ultrafast dynamics in molecules and functional materials.

My work connects custom instrumentation with photophysical analysis, including charge-carrier, exciton, and phonon dynamics in perovskites, organic optoelectronic materials, and narrow-bandgap semiconductors. I also use in situ measurements to follow ultrafast responses while materials undergo slower photochemical or structural changes.

## Research interests

- Asynchronous and interferometric nonlinear spectroscopy (AI-NS)
- Optical-frequency-comb and ultrafast transient absorption spectroscopy
- Charge-carrier, exciton, and phonon dynamics
- Perovskite, organic optoelectronic, and semiconductor materials
- In situ spectroscopy of photoinduced reactions
